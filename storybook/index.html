Chapter III

Red lipstick speaks louder

While studying at MIP in Milan, in 2021, I decided to take part in the L'Oréal Brandstorm challenge, with girls from another university I had met online. We had never been in the same room.

Our team was chosen among the 6 finalists out of 280 in Italy. The prize for the winners: an internship in Paris.

👑

We did not win. (the crown, leaving)

Unfortunately. But we still had the wonderful chance of working side by side for 3 months with a L'Oréal marketing manager to develop the idea, and a final pitch to the L'Oréal Italy Board.

The idea

A Virtual Make-up Masterclass

Customer experience design, before I knew it would become a part of my future job.

Chapter IV · Creative explorations

A little creative detour

After L'Oréal Brandstorm, I wasn't quite ready to leave the beauty world behind. So I started playing around with advertising ideas for brands I loved, creating a few visuals just for fun. No briefs, no clients, no expectations. Just curiosity and a little creative freedom.

Chapter V

A startup called Tell-Me

After that, I worked as a growth marketer for a very nice startup: a travel and lifestyle app where recommendations come from friends you trust.

I sharpened the value proposition and the go-to-market, and ran digital PR before launch.

It was a lovely idea. The market didn't agree.

Lesson: fall in love with the problem, not the product.

Chapter VIII

The Plushy Society

I have always followed my passions. So I wrote a book full of fictional characters: plushies with jobs they're bad at, dreams they're brave about and loves they fumble beautifully.

Then they needed somewhere to live. So I built them a world.

theplushysociety.com is a storybook world for people who believe plushies have a soul. You describe your plushy, and they come to life: they explore places, exchange letters and write to their humans. Plushies meet other plushies, based on recommendations.

Designed, built and launched on my own with Next.js, Supabase and Claude.

50 members and 50+ plushies in the first month.

Side quest

Two ads nobody asked for

I came across a Head of Growth role at Y/n, an interactive fanfiction app. The name is fandom slang for “your name”: you are the main character, and the characters text you back and remember everything. Instead of only sending a CV, I made them four ads, on my own initiative, in about a day: script, voice, editing and motion graphics.

Mid funnel · Lifestyle · 19s

“Morning.”

A character texts her through the day: remembers the “5 more minutes”, asks about her day, keeps her company at lunch.

The bet: people stay for the relationship, not only the plot.
Judge it on: cost per install and day-7 retention.

Top of funnel · Meme · 16s

“Looking for a man who won't ghost you?”

A literal ghost gives dating advice on cardboard signs, then meets a character who replies at 3am. The catch: he's fictional. “…and?”

The bet: dating frustration is a wider door than fanfiction.
Judge it on: hook rate, shares and saves.

Spec work: no brief, no budget, no client. Just the way I'd grow a product I believe in.
